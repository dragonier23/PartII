**Grading Criteria**

| Component | Weight | Criteria |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| **PoS evaluation implementation** | 20% | Correct implementation of the clustering evaluation measures, consistent with their mathematical definitions. The implementation should produce meaningful results and demonstrate a sound understanding of the evaluation methodology. |
| **PoS analysis** | 20% | Comparison of the provided 50d and 100d GloVe embeddings, with clear interpretation of the findings. Higher marks reflect deeper analysis supported by concrete evidence, such as representative cluster contents, word-level comparisons, linguistic patterns or typical errors. Relevant additional experiments may further strengthen the analysis. |
| **Summarisation implementation** | 30% | A coherent and technically sound TextRank implementation, covering graph construction, sentence ranking, summary generation and appropriate evaluation. Implementation choices should be consistent with the intended method and evaluation protocol. |
| **Summarisation analysis** | 30% | Meaningful comparison with suitable baselines and thoughtful discussion of results and limitations. Higher marks generally reflect experimental exploration beyond the core requirements, such as additional models, evaluation measures or datasets, together with well-supported interpretation of the findings. Qualitative comparisons of summary outputs can further strengthen the analysis. |

**Indicative component mark ranges**

These ranges apply to each component according to the relevant criteria above.

| Mark range | General description |
| --- | --- |
| **85–100** | Particularly strong work demonstrating a high standard of correctness, depth or insight. Implementation is accurate and robust; analysis, where assessed, is convincing and supported by appropriate experimental evidence and concrete examples. |
| **70–84** | Good to very good work. Implementation is correct and complete, and analysis provides relevant comparisons and reasonable interpretation. Some limitations in robustness, depth or supporting evidence may remain. |
| **60–69** | Competent work demonstrating understanding of the main methods. Implementation may contain issues affecting part of the results, or analysis may be mainly descriptive with limited supporting investigation. |
| **50–59** | Partial achievement of the component objectives. Significant implementation issues, restricted experimental coverage or underdeveloped analysis limit the contribution. |
| **Below 50** | Substantial errors, omissions or insufficient evidence of understanding. The component is incomplete or its results and conclusions have limited reliability. |
